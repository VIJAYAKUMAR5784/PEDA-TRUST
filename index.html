About PEDA Trust

People-centered development with practical action.

People Education Development Action Trust (PEDA Trust) is a community-oriented trust based in Nagapattinam.

Our work is centered on development and training that can strengthen vulnerable communities, with particular attention to health, environmental awareness and disaster-related preparedness and support.

We believe that knowledge, participation and local action can help people build safer, healthier and more sustainable lives.
